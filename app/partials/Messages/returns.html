Returns & Exchanges Policy

Eligibility Window

  • Returns and exchanges must be requested within 30 days of delivery.
  • Items must be new, unworn, & unwashed.

Non-Returnable Items

  • Worn, washed, soiled, altered, or otherwise damaged garments (outside of shipping defects).
  • Custom name badges are non-returnable.

Return Process

Please reach out to your Uniform Champion and provide them with:

  1. Your Order#
  2. What item is being returned
  3. Reason for return

Your Uniform Champion will process the return and order a replacement piece if needed.

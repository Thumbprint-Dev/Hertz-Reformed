Terms of use

Please read and accept these before ordering your uniform.

This marketplace is for ordering Hertz work uniforms. Items are allocated to you for your role and are provided for use at work.

Order only what you need. Your allocation is yours for the year and unused items stay available to you until it ends.

If something arrives wrong or damaged, start a return rather than reordering — the returned items are credited back to your allocation once the warehouse receives them.

Questions about what you are entitled to go to your Uniform Champion. Questions about an order go to hertzsupport@thumbprint.com.

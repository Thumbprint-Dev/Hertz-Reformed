Frequently Asked Questions

Ordering on Behalf of Employees

How to place orders on behalf of an employee?

Each order must be placed separately for each employee to ensure that their employee ID is being referenced at checkout for reporting purposes.
You can then add the items that are needed into the cart and checkout as normal.
Please click [HERE] for step by step instructions.

What should I do if an employee reaches out to place their uniform order on their behalf because their size is out of stock?

If the size is out of stock we recommend selecting a different size or style. If they need that specific size that is out of stock and you place their order it will go into backorder status.

What is the location# and where do I find it?

The Location# is your 4 digit area number. You will need to use the Location# at checkout to find the location you want to ship to.
NOTE: some Dollar and Thrifty locations are 3 digits but they start with a zero - you will need to reference that zero (EX: 0567).

What happens if an employee should be getting uniforms but they are not a part of the current allocation?

Please reach out to Askoperations@hertz.com with the title of the employee that you believe should be getting uniforms.

How do I order name tags?

Please click [HERE] for step by step instructions on ordering name tags.

What happens if a title is not listed for the Leadership Name Tags?

Please reach out to Askoperations@hertz.com

Not sure which size to choose?

Please be sure you are referencing the correct size charts - measurements are listed in both inches and centimeters.
All measurements are taken flat, not around the body. For example, when measuring hips, measure from side to side rather than around the full circumference.
Reference [THIS] "how to measure" guide for instructions on how to measure yourself.
If you're between sizes, we recommend selecting the larger size for a more comfortable fit.

Where is my order shipping to?

Your orders are being shipped to the location that you select at checkout.

Where is the order shipping to for employees who order via their allocation?

Orders placed during the initial launch will ship in bulk to Uniform Champions for distribution, per the included packing slips.
Orders placed after the initial launch will ship directly to the employees location, addressed to them.
To update a shipping address for a location contact Askoperations@hertz.com

How can I find my tracking for my order?

Log into your account and click on "orders" at the top and you'll be able to click into your order to get the status - if shipped you'll find tracking there.

Returns & Exchanges

How long do I have to request a return or exchange?

You must submit your request within 30 days of receiving your uniform.

Are all items eligible for return?

No. Worn, washed, altered, or damaged items (outside of shipping defects) are not eligible. Also name badges cannot be returned.

What condition must returned items be in?

Items must be new, unworn, & unwashed.

How do I start a return or exchange?

Uniform Champions will submit on behalf of the employee via the online return & exchange request form and provide:
• The Order#
• What item was damaged
• Any images you can share of the damage

Who provides the return shipping label?

When you submit your return/exchange form - Thumbprint Customer Service team will send a return label that can be used for the return.
These returns will be charged to Hertz.

How do I get a replacement item?

Uniform Champions will place replacement item orders on behalf of the employees.

What if my item arrives damaged?

Uniform Champions will submit the damage via the online return & exchange request form and provide:
• Your Order#
• What item was damaged
• Any images you can share of the damage
